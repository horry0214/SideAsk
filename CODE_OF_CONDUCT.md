# SideAsk Community Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

SideAsk welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

Participate with respect. Discuss facts and code, offer actionable feedback, protect user privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or malicious content.

Maintainers may edit or remove issues, comments, and contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel; never disclose personal information in a public issue.
